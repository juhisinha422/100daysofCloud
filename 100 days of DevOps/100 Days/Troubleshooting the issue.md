Sometimes in interviews we get stuck when the interviewer asks, “How do you troubleshoot an issue?

1️⃣ Understand the problem – Check logs and error messages

2️⃣ Check system health – CPU, memory, disk usage

3️⃣ Verify services – Ensure the application or service is running

4️⃣ Check network connectivity – Ping, ports, DNS

5️⃣ Analyze logs – Identify the root cause

6️⃣ Fix and validate – Apply the solution and confirm everything works
