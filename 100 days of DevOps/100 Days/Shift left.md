Finding bugs in production is expensive.

Fixing them during development is smart.

Shift Left means adding testing, security checks, and code validation early in the CI/CD pipeline — not at the end.

Build early. Test early. Secure early. Deploy confidently.

Learning and applying this approach as part of my DevOps journey.
